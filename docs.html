Docs

Everything about Influence in plain words. Short version: your X influence is scored, the score and your actions become points, and the points become your share of the airdrop.

The reward

The reward is the airdrop.

Everything you earn on Influence is Influence Points. At the snapshot, the token airdrop is split among all registered accounts in proportion to their points: twice the points, twice the share. Points are the only input. There is no whitelist, no private allocation and no manual adjustment.

Not announced yet: the total token amount, the snapshot date and the claim mechanics. They will be posted here and on @Influence_rh before the snapshot. Until then, points have no monetary value and no specific token amount is promised.

Genesis Pass

500 passes. The top 500 at snapshot mint free.

The Genesis Pass is an NFT on Robinhood Chain, limited to 500, minted once and never again. At the snapshot, the 500 accounts with the most Influence Points qualify to mint one, free, with their linked wallet. One pass per account. Nothing is sold.

How to qualify: connect X, finish the join checklist, invite people, complete the daily tasks, win Influence Battles. Rank 500 or better at the snapshot is the only condition.

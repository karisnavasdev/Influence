The round

Two accounts. One theme. One post each.

  1. 01Challenge anyone by username, or post an open challenge. Stake from 100 points, or play a friendly battle with no stake.
  2. 02When the other side accepts, both of you have 24 hours to publish one original post on the theme.
  3. 03Score is engagement divided by followers. A smaller account that gets people talking can beat a larger one.
  4. 04Winner takes the pot minus a 5% treasury fee. No post means forfeit. A draw returns both stakes.
  5. 05Spectators can back a side while the window is open. Winning backers share the losing pool.

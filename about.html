How it works

The promise

Your points are your allocation.

At the snapshot, the airdrop is split in proportion to Influence Points. Points are the only input: no whitelist, no hand-picked wallets, no separate deal for anyone. The token amount and the snapshot date are not announced yet; the rule is.

The formula

Four signals, weighted in the open.

Your score is built from public X data only. It converts to Influence Points and an estimated dollar value. If X does not return a signal, that signal is left out rather than counted as zero.

35%Engagement. Interactions per post relative to followers.
20%Reach. Average impressions on recent original posts.
15%Followers, on a log scale.
10%Content quality. Conversation over passive taps.
10%Network. Who you bring in and how they do.
10%Account age. Under 30 days counts as nothing.
